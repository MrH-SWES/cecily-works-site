Cecily Works

Private personal-assistant integration

Cecily Works is a private, single-user application used by its owner to connect personal Google Workspace data to a self-hosted assistant environment.

What it does

With the owner's consent, Cecily Works can use Google OAuth to work with services such as Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts, and Tasks in support of actions the owner requests.

Who uses it

This is not a public SaaS product and is not offered for general public use. Access is limited to the owner and any explicitly authorized personal accounts.

Privacy

For details about how Google user data is handled, see the Privacy Policy.
