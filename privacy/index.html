Privacy Policy

Cecily Works

Cecily Works is a private, single-user personal-assistant integration operated for its owner's personal use.

Google user data accessed

When the owner authorizes access through Google OAuth, Cecily Works may access Google services including Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts, and Tasks, limited to the scopes the owner has explicitly granted.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to perform actions or provide information requested by the owner through the Cecily assistant environment.

Storage

OAuth credentials and any locally retained application data are stored in the owner's private, self-hosted environment. They are not published through this website.

Sharing and sale

Cecily Works does not sell Google user data. Google user data is not shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls the connected Google account, OAuth authorization, and local self-hosted data. Access can be revoked through the Google account at any time, and locally stored application data can be removed by the owner.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to cecilyrowan.works@gmail.com.
